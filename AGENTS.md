# Clean Take

Clean Take is a trainer for guitar and piano. It is a local Vite + TypeScript app with no UI framework and two pages: the guitar course (Trainer, `/`, `src/trainer/`) and Pieces (learn a specific piece on piano or guitar, `/piece.html`, `src/piece/`). Both listen through the microphone and grade each take honestly; Pieces also takes computer keys or MIDI. The product name is set once in `src/brand.ts` and in the two HTML files. The latest product contract is at the top of `SPEC.md`; `CODEX_PROMPT.md` is the original build prompt and is mostly history.

Partner and Looper (the former Play page, `/play.html`) were removed on 2026-11-22 at the owner's request. Their code is in git history and their designs are in the historical sections of `SPEC.md`. The recorder they introduced stays, because every take is recorded through it (see the recording rule below).

## Rules

- Pieces (`/piece.html`, `src/piece/`) teaches a specific piece on piano or guitar. Piece files are plain text compiled by `notation.ts`; analysis, curriculum, drills and the guitar arrangement are deterministic code. Committed packs must be `redistributable: yes`; private music goes in git-ignored `src/piece/packs/local/` or the app's local import. Read PIECES.md before adding a piece: transcribe twice (printed and sounding modes), diff with `scripts/piece-check.mjs`, then listen.
- Adding a piece: the owner's own pieces come in through the import area in the Pieces menu (`add-panel.ts`, `add-view.ts`, `import.ts`, `mine.ts`). The app gives instructions for any AI chat (`import-guide.ts`; its examples, tokens, staff table and stated range are tested against `notation.ts`), reads the answer back as untrusted text, says what is wrong in words the owner can paste back to the AI, and compares two readings (`compare.ts`, shared with `scripts/piece-check.mjs`). Apart from the optional AI reader, the app itself calls no model and makes no network request for any of this. The AI reader (`reader.ts`, `pages.ts`, `pdf.ts`, `reader-view.ts`, loaded only when its part of the panel is opened; the owner approved it on 2026-11-23) sends, only when the owner presses Read, the page pictures he chose with the same instructions (`importGuide`) and his own key, straight from the browser to the chat service he named, and puts the answer, as untrusted text, into the same box and through the same check, problems, blocking rules and draft state as a pasted answer: never a second path. Cutting pages into pictures is best effort: every sentence about the pictures, on the page or sent to the service, must be true whatever the cut, no content of a chosen page may be left out silently, and no text may claim that the reading is accurate. With preview running, `node scripts/reader-smoke.mjs` drives it against a fake service; no test or script ever calls a real one. When reading an answer back, the app may tidy characters where the format needs plain ones and must say what it did; it never changes the words of the title, composer, source or licence, never guesses at music, and never drops or adds piece lines silently: a problem with how the text was pasted (a part missing, repeated or out of order, a stray line outside a code block) is told to the owner in a plain sentence and blocks adding, and is not sent to the AI. A piece the owner added is a draft (`verified: none` or `checks` in its own text) until his own "It sounds right" click sets `verified: owner`; comparing readings, the AI's own claims and anything automatic never confirm a piece. With preview running, `node scripts/import-smoke.mjs` drives the whole panel in real Chrome.
- Piano listening (`listen.ts`, `grade.ts`) is score-informed and must stay honest: heard needs a note's own low partials to rise, including an odd one; not heard needs them quiet (or decaying after a 0.5 s gap) while the attack clearly sounded; octave/twelfth doublings are "can't check"; everything else is unclear and never counts. Attacks need a real rise (three bands: confirmed, unsure, none). Thresholds were set on MAESTRO dev excerpts and frozen; re-measure on held-out real recordings before changing them, and report false confirmation and false contradiction rates.
- Pieces also has a Keyboard · MIDI input for piano (`keys.ts`, `gradeKeys`): computer keys (Z and Q rows, tracker layout) or Web MIDI, graded from exact key presses on the audio clock with no microphone and no calibration. The two key rows are placed per step (`layoutFor`), split apart when the music spreads; notes they cannot reach are played by the app and not graded, and the result says so (MIDI keyboards reach everything). Keys takes keep presses, not audio, and replay on the piano voice. Wait for me (`wait.ts`) checks keys only and never moves the tempo ladder.
- Pieces motivation contracts: reward effects (flashes, sparks, milestone moments, celebrations) appear only for confirmed results; the microphone never gives live per-note judgement during a take (live key feedback is keys/MIDI only, using the grade's own rules); only trusted takes are celebrated, counted as clean or kept as bests; the comfort answer is always the learner's own key press or click (ArrowUp / ArrowDown / Space, never a piano key, never automatic). The daily plan (`suggestToday`) walks one phrase at a time with a second-day slot, capped due reviews and at most two due weak passages; it never changes the ladder. Spot practice (`spot.ts`) loops any bar range of a step and can step its tempo up or down inside the loop; trusted takes remember recurring bars as at most five weak spots per course, which return on their due day as an ordinary passage, never as a verdict. Placement (`canPlace`, `placeUnit`, `placeHands` in `progress.ts`) is the only way to skip the ladder: an explicit graded take from the placement control at a chosen tempo step moves the step there only when trusted, calibrated, 80% or better (the clean bar, `CLEAN` in `progress.ts`) and comfortable, counts as its first clean take, never certifies or marks learned, and records nothing on a miss; free-tempo and Wait for me takes never place; one-hand steps cap at 80%. The rhythm layer (`log`, `dailyGoal`, `today` inside `piece.trainer.v1`) is lenient: count clean work, nothing resets to zero, no loss framing, no points for time, for takes played, for days in a row or for opening the page, no day streaks and no notifications. The game layer (stars, points, levels, achievements; `game.ts`) is allowed on these terms: it is earned only from counted results (a trusted take, calibrated where the tempo step needs it, with the learner's own comfort answer where the ladder needs it) and from saved progress that never decreases; unclear or unscored input earns nothing, costs nothing and shows neither stars nor a percentage; nothing earned is removed except by the learner's own reset; stars, combos and points are read-outs of the grade and never change the score, the clean rule, the ladder or the plan; the in-take combo counter is keys/MIDI only and ends quietly on a wrong key. Take stars on the result card are such a read-out: they appear wherever a trusted take's percentage appears, including takes that do not count toward the ladder (free tempo, timing not yet calibrated), and they earn nothing by themselves; step stars come only from saved progress. The mastery map shows only saved progress. The journey map (`journey.ts`, `journey-view.ts`) and the Finish summary (`summary.ts`) are read-outs too and store nothing: the map shows every step exactly once from saved progress, with nothing locked and nothing shown as lost, and it moves only in the lively look; the summary compares saved progress with a copy taken when the page loaded, counts only answers the ladder counted, and never mentions a step down.
- Pieces piano sound: the default is a sampled grand (`src/piece/sampler.ts`, University of Iowa Steinway recordings in `public/piano/`, reproduced by `npm run vendor:piano` and checked by `npm run check:piano`); "Simple" is the oscillator voice. Samples load from this origin only after a user gesture and only for the piece's range; until ready, the simple voice plays. A take, demo or Wait for me run keeps the voice it started with. Playback starts at each buffer's measured onset so attacks land on the planned time; no compressor or look-ahead on the path. Metronome clicks, pips and the guitar voice stay synthesized.
- Pieces lane light: `light.ts` (`lightFrame`) is the single pure decision of what reward light exists each frame (key glows and rings, particle bursts, the warmth band, the beat glow, the reveal sweep, celebrations); none in Calm, with reduced motion, or during a microphone take or demo. `lightgl.ts` draws it with WebGL2 on an additive overlay (`.piece-light`) clipped above the hit line and kept off text boxes; `drawLight2d` in `view.ts` draws the same frame when WebGL2 is missing or lost, or with `?light=2d` (`?light=gl` forces WebGL). Ink (notes, keys, labels, the hit line and its beat alpha, the early/late tick, the microphone level band) stays on the 2D canvas. Limits: 150 live particles during a take, 1536 in all.
- The guitar course (Trainer, `src/trainer/`) is the landing page. The approved Trainer contract at the top of SPEC.md allows lessons, fret/string guidance and local progress. Use CREPE for single-note grading, explicitly label rhythm-only chord work, and never grade uncertain input as success or failure: a take that cannot be judged shows "Not scored" with its reason, no percentages and no note marks (`src/trainer/result.ts`). Adaptive tempo changes only between attempts.
- CREPE is the pitch ear and must actually run as a tfjs Layers model from committed `public/models/` assets. Never substitute YIN as the default.
- Attacks are note-on events, not ringing energy. Confidence dropouts and octave blips must not invent attacks or rests.
- The tempo comes from the lesson, the tempo ladder or the learner (typed, or tapped with Space in the guitar course while stopped). No tempo estimation from playing, and playing never retimes the pulse.
- Schedule musical events with `AudioContext.currentTime` minus `outputLatency`.
- No generative model runs in the app or on its server, and nothing is sent to one, with one exception: the optional AI reader in the import area (see "Adding a piece"), on the owner's own action and with his own key. That key lives in `piece.reader.v1` in his browser only: it is not a progress key, is never backed up or synced, goes only in the Authorization header to the one address it was typed for, never in a URL, a body, a message or a log, and is forgotten when the address moves to another origin. Audio is never sent to any model. The app is offline-first and has no active backend: accounts and cross-device sync are switched off (`accountsEnabled = false` in `src/account/config.ts`), and no `/api/*` request starts at runtime. The historical `src/account/`, `api/`, `server/`, `db/` and `tests/server/` code remains in the repository for reference, not as a supported path. Progress and local pieces move between browsers only through the explicit JSON backup (`src/storage/backup.ts`): `clean-take-backup-YYYY-MM-DD.json` carries `guitar.trainer.v1`, `piece.trainer.v1`, `piece.imported.v1` and `piece.input.v1`, never `piece.reader.v1` or `guitar.sync.v1`; import validates every part before writing, offers Merge or Replace, and rolls back every written key on a storage error. localStorage stays the working copy and signed-out use must keep working with no network. Any new localStorage progress key must be added to `BACKUP_KEYS` in `src/storage/backup.ts` (and to its validator) so a backup carries it; the old sync allowlists are historical. Trainer uses authored exercises and local progress. The former Play page's settings key `guitar.practice.v1` is no longer written by the app and is not part of progress or backups.
- Stop releases the microphone and silences audio, including a pending microphone request. Protect against Stop racing a worklet completion message.
- Never store or upload captured audio or notes automatically. Audio stays in memory until reload; downloads are explicit and local.
- Complete each phase and run its tests before the next. Verify Chrome with actual CREPE and annotated recorded guitar input before publishing. Do not claim that automated fixtures establish physical-room reliability.
- Recording: every take is recorded by a dedicated native-rate AudioWorklet on sample-clock boundaries (`src/audio/looper.worklet.ts` with `src/audio/loop-engine.ts`, kept from the former Looper; `loop-level.ts` and `loop-wav.ts` serve playback level and WAV export). These files are not part of the removed Play page.

## Commands

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
npm test
npm run build
npm run preview
npm run verify
```

`npm run verify` runs the format check (Prettier), the tests and the production build. With preview running, `node scripts/trainer-smoke.mjs` and `node scripts/piece-smoke.mjs` drive real Chrome with recorded guitar and piano input, and `node scripts/import-smoke.mjs` drives the import area (no audio; `SMOKE_SIZE=390x844` for phone width); set `SMOKE_ORIGIN` to the preview URL. See README for options.

## Layout

- `src/trainer/` is the guitar course: `audio.ts` records and analyses a take, `grader.ts` grades it, `result.ts` decides what the result shows, `progress.ts` adapts the tempo.
- `src/piece/` is Pieces: `notation.ts` compiles a piece file; `curriculum.ts`, `guitar.ts` and `solo.ts` build the courses; `listen.ts` and `grade.ts` grade a take; `progress.ts` and `loop.ts` run the tempo ladder; `game.ts` is the game layer; `journey.ts` and `journey-view.ts` are the Course panel's journey map and `summary.ts` is the Finish summary; `import-guide.ts`, `import.ts`, `compare.ts`, `mine.ts`, `add-view.ts` and `add-panel.ts` are the import area, and `reader.ts`, `pages.ts`, `pdf.ts` and `reader-view.ts` its optional AI reader; `main.ts` and `view.ts` are the page.
- `src/audio/` is shared: `attack.ts` and `onset.ts` segment plucks, `ear.ts` and `crepe.ts` read pitch, `looper.worklet.ts` and `loop-engine.ts` record takes.
- `src/account/` with `api/` and `server/` is the historical account/sync code, switched off in this offline-first build (no runtime network calls); JSON backup lives in `src/storage/backup.ts`.
- Models are committed under `public/models/` and piano recordings under `public/piano/`; test recording provenance is under `tests/fixtures/`.
