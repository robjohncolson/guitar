# Guitar Practice Partner

Local Vite + TypeScript app with three pages: Trainer (guitar course, `/`), Pieces (learn a specific piece on piano or guitar, `/piece.html`) and Play (Partner and Looper, `/play.html`). In Partner, enter BPM, play a repeating idea, and hear three finite Echo or Harmony replies half a beat later. Harmony is the default and falls back to a soft octave when uncertain. The latest product contract is at the top of `SPEC.md`; implementation constraints and phase order are in `CODEX_PROMPT.md`.

The new Looper mode records four bars of native-rate mono audio in 4/4 after a one-bar count-in, then loops continuously. Replace waits for a loop boundary and silences the old take during recording. Stop retains the last completed take and releases the microphone; Play restores that take's tempo. One Undo restores the previous take; Clear/reload discard audio. Partner and Looper never play together. Read the current Looper contract in SPEC.md before changing either mode.

Listen continuously for three complete matching cycles, including internal and closing rests. Use tolerant note/time alignment, not a four-beat window, note-count gate, or pitch histogram. Preserve timing and rests in both modes; Echo preserves detected pitch and accepted attack dynamics, while Harmony chooses supporting pitches and softer dynamics. Keep listening during playback with one waiting loop; app voices and phrases must not overlap. Continuing the same idea cannot requeue it forever. Start fresh / L clears evidence and replies while keeping the clock.

## Rules

- Pieces (`/piece.html`, `src/piece/`) teaches a specific piece on piano or guitar. Piece files are plain text compiled by `notation.ts`; analysis, curriculum, drills and the guitar arrangement are deterministic code. Committed packs must be `redistributable: yes`; private music goes in git-ignored `src/piece/packs/local/` or the app's local import. Read PIECES.md before adding a piece: transcribe twice (printed and sounding modes), diff with `scripts/piece-check.mjs`, then listen.
- Piano listening (`listen.ts`, `grade.ts`) is score-informed and must stay honest: heard needs a note's own low partials to rise, including an odd one; not heard needs them quiet (or decaying after a 0.5 s gap) while the attack clearly sounded; octave/twelfth doublings are "can't check"; everything else is unclear and never counts. Attacks need a real rise (three bands: confirmed, unsure, none). Thresholds were set on MAESTRO dev excerpts and frozen; re-measure on held-out real recordings before changing them, and report false confirmation and false contradiction rates.
- Pieces also has a Keyboard · MIDI input for piano (`keys.ts`, `gradeKeys`): computer keys (Z and Q rows, tracker layout) or Web MIDI, graded from exact key presses on the audio clock with no microphone and no calibration. The two key rows are placed per step (`layoutFor`), split apart when the music spreads; notes they cannot reach are played by the app and not graded, and the result says so (MIDI keyboards reach everything). Keys takes keep presses, not audio, and replay on the piano voice. Wait for me (`wait.ts`) checks keys only and never moves the tempo ladder.
- Pieces motivation contracts: reward effects (flashes, sparks, milestone moments, celebrations) appear only for confirmed results; the microphone never gives live per-note judgement during a take (live key feedback is keys/MIDI only, using the grade's own rules); only trusted takes are celebrated, counted as clean or kept as bests; the comfort answer is always the learner's own key press or click (ArrowUp / ArrowDown / Space, never a piano key, never automatic). The daily plan (`suggestToday`) walks one phrase at a time with a second-day slot and capped due reviews; it never changes the ladder. Placement (`canPlace`, `placeUnit`, `placeHands` in `progress.ts`) is the only way to skip the ladder: an explicit graded take from the placement control at a chosen tempo step moves the step there only when trusted, calibrated, 90% or better and comfortable, counts as its first clean take, never certifies or marks learned, and records nothing on a miss; free-tempo and Wait for me takes never place; one-hand steps cap at 80%. The rhythm layer (`log`, `dailyGoal`, `today` inside `piece.trainer.v1`) is lenient: count clean work, nothing resets to zero, no loss framing, no points for time or for opening the page, no streaks, badges, XP or notifications. The mastery map shows only saved progress.
- Pieces piano sound: the default is a sampled grand (`src/piece/sampler.ts`, University of Iowa Steinway recordings in `public/piano/`, reproduced by `npm run vendor:piano` and checked by `npm run check:piano`); "Simple" is the oscillator voice. Samples load from this origin only after a user gesture and only for the piece's range; until ready, the simple voice plays. A take, demo or Wait for me run keeps the voice it started with. Playback starts at each buffer's measured onset so attacks land on the planned time; no compressor or look-ahead on the path. Metronome clicks, pips and the guitar voice stay synthesized.
- Trainer is now the landing page, with the existing Partner/Looper on the Play page. The approved Trainer contract at the top of SPEC.md allows lessons, fret/string guidance and local progress. Use CREPE for single-note grading, explicitly label rhythm-only chord work, and never grade uncertain input as success or failure. Adaptive tempo changes only between attempts.
- CREPE is the pitch ear and must actually run as a tfjs Layers model from committed `public/models/` assets. Never substitute YIN as the default.
- Occupancy is note-on attacks, not ringing energy. Confidence dropouts and octave blips must not invent attacks or rests. Masked app notes must not cut up player sustains.
- Manual BPM or Space tapping sets the clock. No guitar BPM estimation. The first accepted pluck anchors phase at the entered tempo; subsequent playing never retimes the pulse.
- Offbeat means the whole loop shifted by half an entered beat, preserving its captured pace. No bar rounding or downbeat entry wait.
- Harmony chooses one smooth line from the whole source loop and the expected guitar at each delayed landing. Keep the line fixed for three passes; soften the third. Mode changes apply to the next exchange. Recognition compares source notes; masking uses actual output notes.
- Schedule musical events with `AudioContext.currentTime` minus `outputLatency`.
- No generative models. The only backend is the optional account API (`api/`, `server/`, README "Optional accounts"): it stores synced progress and opted-in pieces, never audio. localStorage stays the working copy and signed-out use must keep working with no network. Any new localStorage progress key must be added to `src/account/config.ts`, the server allowlist (`server/core.ts`) and the `guitar.progress` CHECK, or it will not sync. Trainer uses authored exercises and local progress. Partner Harmony uses deterministic phrase-level pitch selection, one note at a time; see the current additions in SPEC.md.
- Stop releases the microphone and silences audio, including a pending microphone request. Partner starts fresh; Looper retains its completed take and Undo history in this page. Protect against Stop racing a worklet completion message.
- Minimal primary controls: BPM, Reply: Echo / Harmony, one pulse dot, state, and Stop. Keep optional settings and the explicit local 20-second listening check collapsed. Never store or upload captured audio/notes automatically.
- North-star demo: repeat a five-beat A-C-E idea three times with pauses; hear C-E-G in Harmony or A-C-E in Echo a half beat later for three passes, then quiet. Learn a different loop during playback and let it follow without overlap.
- Complete each phase and run its tests before the next. Verify Chrome with actual CREPE and annotated recorded guitar input before publishing. Do not claim that automated fixtures establish physical-room reliability.
- Looper capture/playback uses a dedicated native-rate AudioWorklet and sample-clock boundaries. The diagnostic 16 kHz / 20-second recorder is not its recording path. No pitch gate, overdubbing, or raw-audio speaker-bleed removal. Optional input-level softening is for headphones and does not alter saved audio.

## Commands

```bash
npm install
npm run dev
npm test
npm run build
npm run preview
```

With preview running, `node scripts/browser-smoke.mjs` uses recorded guitar plucks; set `SMOKE_ORIGIN` to the preview URL. See README for options.

## Layout

`src/audio/attack.ts` and `onset.ts` segment plucks; `src/music/motif.ts` recognizes loops and rests; `reply.ts` schedules three passes; `companion.ts` owns continuous input, masking, and one waiting loop; `src/audio/scheduler.ts` schedules output. `listening-capture.ts` supports the opt-in local check. Models are committed under `public/models/`; test recording provenance is under `tests/fixtures/`.
