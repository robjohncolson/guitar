## Known limits

- The first-run welcome card's list is clamped to one line on a phone, so its steps 2 to 4 cannot be read there (the card is account code; only its CSS under `.piece` changed). The clamp is deliberate: with two lines the sticky block overlapped the lane at 390x844.
- Desktop windows shorter than about 740 px of page (a 1366x768 laptop leaves about 650 px; Start ends at 714 there, 64 px below the fold) put Start below the fold, because the desktop lane never goes under 300 px (250 with a card or NOTICE). The floor is kept.
- At 390x664 a tall NOTICE (for example the blocked microphone, three lines and a button) leaves less than 100 px of lane above the sticky block; NOTICE and Start stay visible and scrolling reveals the lane. The measured numbers are in the Round 4 entry of UX-REDESIGN.md.
